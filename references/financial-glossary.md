# Financial Glossary

## BIC / SWIFT
A BIC (Business Identifier Code), commonly called a SWIFT code, identifies a business party/institution in financial messaging and routing contexts. BIC structure is standardized by ISO 9362. Always distinguish code syntax from current operational status.

## IBAN
International Bank Account Number. A standardized representation of an account number used in participating countries/markets. Country structure and length are country-specific under ISO 13616.

## ABA Routing Number / Routing Number
A U.S. bank routing identifier used for specific payment rails. Do not assume one routing number applies to every payment type or the same bank account relationship.

## Sort Code
A UK banking sort identifier, commonly six digits, used in UK domestic payment contexts. International payments can involve IBAN/BIC instead or as well.

## IFSC
Indian Financial System Code, used to identify an Indian bank branch for domestic electronic payment systems.

## BSB
Australian bank-state-branch identifier used for Australian domestic payments.

## Beneficiary
The person or organization receiving the funds.

## Beneficiary Bank
The bank that holds the beneficiary account or is designated to receive the funds.

## Intermediary Bank
A bank that may route a payment between the sending and receiving bank when a direct relationship/route is not used or not available.

## Correspondent Bank
A bank that provides services for another bank, often including payment clearing or settlement across currencies/markets.

## OUR / SHA / BEN
Common fee-allocation labels for cross-border payments:
- OUR: sender bears charges according to the payment instruction, subject to bank/correspondent rules.
- SHA: sender and beneficiary generally bear their respective bank charges.
- BEN: charges are generally deducted from the amount received by the beneficiary.
Exact treatment varies by bank, corridor, currency, and payment system.

## Payment Purpose
A description of the legitimate purpose of a payment.

## Remittance Information / Payment Reference
Text or structured information used to identify the underlying payment, invoice, contract, or reason for transfer.
